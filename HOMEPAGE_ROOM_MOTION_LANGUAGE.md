# Homepage Room Motion Language

Each room speaks through a distinct vocabulary of movement. The motions are not decorative — they are how the room introduces itself before the visitor reads a word. Every room should feel like it has its own physics, its own sense of time, and its own reason for moving.

Across all five rooms, motion should feel **authored, quiet, and confident**. Nothing bounces for attention. Nothing rushes to impress. The visitor should sense that the movement belongs to the object — envelope, ink, lens, record, landscape — not to the interface.

---

## The Mail Room

**Motion metaphor:** Correspondence arriving by hand.

The Mail Room moves like paper handled by a person who has done this many times — deliberate, unhurried, slightly intimate. Motion here is **contained, sequential, and tactile**. Things open. Things slide. Things are revealed in the order they would be in real life.

### Vocabulary

| Quality | Language |
|---|---|
| **Verbs** | Open, lift, slide, unfold, settle |
| **Rhythm** | Measured pauses between beats — the breath before an envelope is opened |
| **Weight** | Light paper, slight friction, soft landing |
| **Direction** | Mostly horizontal emergence; vertical only as lift, not jump |
| **Duration** | Slow enough to feel intentional; never instant |

### What the motion communicates

Someone wrote this. Someone sealed it. Someone is now letting you in. The movement should carry the strange intimacy of reading mail that was meant for another person — private, but offered.

### Signature gestures

- The envelope opens from a single point of decision, not from spectacle.
- The letter slides out with the quiet confidence of something already written — it is not being generated, it is being **presented**.
- The text appears as if it was always inside; the motion merely exposes what was waiting.

### What this room never does

- No post office mechanics, no rubber-stamp comedy, no fluttering papers.
- No notification energy, no chat bubbles, no social feed urgency.
- No bounce, snap, or elastic overshoot — correspondence is not a game.

## Journal

**Motion metaphor:** Ink finding its way onto the page.

The Journal moves like thought becoming visible — **downward, organic, irreversible**. Where the Mail Room is sequential and paper-bound, the Journal is fluid and gravitational. Motion here follows the logic of writing: one line leads to the next, curiosity pulls you deeper, slowing is the point.

### Vocabulary

| Quality | Language |
|---|---|
| **Verbs** | Fall, extend, bleed, trace, pool, continue |
| **Rhythm** | A single sustained gesture rather than a sequence of clicks |
| **Weight** | Liquid, absorbed, patient |
| **Direction** | Downward pull — gravity and attention working together |
| **Duration** | Longer than the Mail Room; motion should ask the visitor to stay |

### What the motion communicates

This room asks you to slow down. The ink drop is not an effect — it is the first sentence deciding to exist. The writing does not arrive all at once; it **becomes**, the way a long-form thought becomes clear only after you have sat with it.

### Signature gestures

- A drop extends downward and transforms into language — one continuous act, not a cut between image and text.
- The text reads as handwritten in temperament even when set in type: motion should feel **drawn**, not typed.
- Stillness after the gesture matters — the page holds.

### What this room never does

- No typewriter clicking, no cursor blinking, no feed-scroll velocity.
- No aggressive scribbling or frantic handwriting energy.
- No spectacle around grief — the origin of this room is serious; motion should be reverent, not dramatic.

## NERV

**Motion metaphor:** Focus moving across a surface until pattern appears.

NERV is the only room whose motion is **exploratory and responsive**. It does not perform at the visitor — it waits for the visitor to look closer. The magnifying lens is not a hover effect; it is a method of seeing. Motion here is **radial, investigative, and revealing** — the opposite of the Journal's downward gravity and the Mail Room's horizontal unfold.

### Vocabulary

| Quality | Language |
|---|---|
| **Verbs** | Magnify, drift, resolve, uncover, sharpen, expand |
| **Rhythm** | Follows the visitor's attention — responsive, not autonomous |
| **Weight** | Optical, precise, glass-and-light |
| **Direction** | Radiates from the point of focus; expansion outward when the lens commits |
| **Duration** | Continuous while exploring; decisive when transitioning into the room |

### What the motion communicates

Behavior has a pattern beneath it. What looked like surface detail becomes structure when you look long enough. NERV teaches the visitor to **examine before they conclude** — the lens is the room's philosophy made visible.

### Signature gestures

- The lens moves as a true instrument of observation — what it passes over changes in clarity, not in cartoon highlight.
- Hidden copy resolves inside the field of focus, as though it was always there and only needed attention.
- When the lens commits, it expands with the calm certainty of understanding — not a zoom for thrill, but a door opening because the pattern has been recognized.

### What this room never does

- No clinical scanning, no diagnostic UI, no test-result reveals.
- No magnifying-glass novelty bounce, no Sherlock Holmes theatrics.
- No motion that implies judgment — NERV observes; it does not pronounce.

## Music

**Motion metaphor:** Rotation and resonance.

Music moves the way a record moves — **circular, continuous, emotionally centered**. The visitor encounters the record already spinning with the quiet continuity of an object behaving according to its nature. That sustained rotation gives the closed composition its living presence. Discovery occurs when the visitor opens the album, shifting attention from the rotating record to the designed interior waiting inside.

### Vocabulary

| Quality | Language |
|---|---|
| **Verbs** | Spin, rotate, open, reveal, resonate, settle |
| **Rhythm** | Cyclical and steady — a pulse you feel more than count |
| **Weight** | Warm, physical, analog |
| **Direction** | Circular while the record turns; opening outward when the album is discovered |
| **Duration** | The spin is ongoing; the album opening is a physical reveal, not a climax |

### What the motion communicates

Some understanding arrives as melody before it arrives as language. Music is the room where feeling leads. The motion should feel **personal and strangely specific** — as if the record was chosen for you, not broadcast to everyone.

### Signature gestures

- The visitor encounters the record already turning with the unselfconscious continuity of something already playing — not a DJ drop.
- The visitor directly opens the album cover, immediately exposing the complete Music text and **LISTEN.** as part of one cohesive interior artwork.
- Motion and stillness coexist — rotation gives the closed composition life; once the album opens, the interior is allowed to hold attention without motion competing with the text.

### What this room never does

- No equalizer bars, no waveform theatrics, no streaming-platform energy.
- No aggressive bass-drop timing, no strobe, no concert-flash spectacle.
- No motion that treats music as background ambiance — here, music is the subject.

## Experiences

**Motion metaphor:** A place breathing while you arrive.

Experiences is the only room whose motion is **environmental and ambient** — the world inside the poster is alive before the visitor touches anything. Where other rooms move to reveal copy, Experiences moves to reveal **atmosphere**: light shifting, plants swaying, water catching sun, objects resting in a lived-in scene. This is not a card animating; it is a **place existing**.

### Vocabulary

| Quality | Language |
|---|---|
| **Verbs** | Sway, drift, glimmer, shift, breathe, separate |
| **Rhythm** | Loose and overlapping — many small motions, none dominating |
| **Weight** | Air, sunlight, fabric, foliage — light as weather, not light as effect |
| **Direction** | Multi-directional but harmonized; nothing moves on a grid |
| **Duration** | Continuous and subtle; reveal gestures are slow and layered |

### What the motion communicates

An experience is not a rating — it is a place you enter with your whole temperament. The poster holds a world that was actually lived. Motion here should feel **documentary, not designed** — the way a porch sways or light moves across a table when you sit long enough to notice.

### Signature gestures

- Folded correspondence tucked inside the woven bag opens naturally to reveal the blurb, while the environment continues living around it.
- Environmental motion continues beneath and beyond the reveal — the scene does not stop when copy appears.
- Light and movement imply time passing: morning becoming afternoon, breeze, reflection — not looped gimmick.

### What this room never does

- No theme-park motion, no travel-ad kinetic type, no airplane/map/f luggage iconography in movement.
- No hyperactive parallax that makes the visitor feel sold to.
- No motion that suggests the experience belongs to someone else's taste rather than the visitor's own NERVprint.

## How the rooms relate to one another

The five motion languages should feel like five ways of paying attention:

| Room | Attention mode | Motion character |
|---|---|---|
| **Mail Room** | Receive | Sequential, tactile, intimate |
| **Journal** | Slow down | Gravitational, fluid, becoming |
| **NERV** | Examine | Responsive, optical, revealing |
| **Music** | Feel | Circular, resonant, centered |
| **Experiences** | Arrive | Ambient, environmental, breathing |

NERV is the lens through which the others can be re-seen — its motion language (focus, resolve, expand) is the site's grammar of understanding. The Portal signature remains quiet throughout the transition into the Homepage. It is not animated for spectacle; it simply finds its place. The rooms introduce motion only after the visitor has crossed from Manifesto into Homepage. Each room's first movement should feel like the natural next sentence, not an animation turning on.

---

## Shared principles (all rooms)

1. **Object-led motion** — The envelope, ink, lens, record, and landscape move; the UI does not perform.
2. **Quiet confidence** — No bounce, glow, flourish, or spectacle as default vocabulary.
3. **Reveal, don't announce** — Motion exposes what was already there.
4. **Distinct physics** — A visitor who scrolls through all five rooms should feel five different temperaments of movement, not one reused interaction pattern with different assets.
5. **Earned motion** — Movement intensifies only when the visitor's attention warrants it (especially NERV); ambient rooms breathe whether or not anyone is watching (Experiences).

This is the motion language each room should speak when animations are eventually authored. The implementation can vary; the vocabulary should not.
