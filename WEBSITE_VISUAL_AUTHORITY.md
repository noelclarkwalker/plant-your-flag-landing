3. **`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`** governs **global brand and design-system principles and tokens**, although portions of that document still require later reconciliation with this authority.

5. **Room-specific treatments** govern room-specific creative and experiential behavior where they do not conflict with higher authority.

6. **Engineering** must faithfully implement these authorities and may not invent new product or visual decisions.

If this document and a higher authority genuinely conflict, **record the conflict** rather than silently resolving it in implementation.

This document must not override, redefine, expand, or silently resolve anything governed or deliberately left unresolved by the Product Model or Site Interaction Language.

## 3. GLOBAL APPROVED VISUAL DIRECTION

The following global visual direction is **approved** for Version 1:

- Contemporary rather than vintage, antique, archival-museum, or period-room design.
- Bright white and black foundation.
- Controlled high-energy use of brand color as punctuation:
  - purple `#A21EFF`
  - pink `#FF2D78`
  - turquoise `#00BFBF`
  - yellow `#FFEE08`
- **Balgih** is the approved major display/headline voice.
- **Bebas Neue** is the approved subheading/label/emphasis voice.
- Exact readable body typeface remains an implementation/design-system decision unless already governed elsewhere.
- Large expressive NC monogram.
- Paint/ink gestures coexist with disciplined digital structure.
- Clean digital architecture + human marks + physical artifacts.
- Historical correspondence may visibly carry age.
- The NoelClark.com interface itself must remain contemporary.

**Governing principle:**

> **The age belongs to the artifact, not the interface.**

A historical letter may look historical.

NoelClark.com itself remains contemporary.

## 4. HOMEPAGE — APPROVED VISUAL COMPOSITION

The approved Homepage visual direction visible in the locked mockup includes:

- **NOELCLARK.COM** identity at upper left.
- Primary navigation includes:
  - **THE MAIL ROOM**
  - **P.S.**
  - **ABOUT**
  - **JOHN CLARK**
  - **CONTACT**
- Membership/access is visible but secondary.
- Large editorial statement/display area.
- Two dominant primary destination doors:
  - **THE MAIL ROOM**
  - **PERSONAL SEAL, P.S.**
- The Mail Room is represented by concentrated purple field/envelope language.
- Personal Seal, P.S. is represented by concentrated hot-pink field/P.S. object language.
- The two doors are peers but do **not** need to be mechanically identical.
- A black anchoring footer/utility region completes the page.
- Overall page rhythm:
  - white editorial/hero field
  - → concentrated two-door destination field
  - → black anchoring footer/utility field
- The concentrated two-door destination field contains the peer Mail Room and Personal Seal, P.S. visual territories, including the approved purple and hot-pink language.

**Important:** The exact generated slogan/editorial copy visible in the mockup is **not** automatically approved merely because it appears in the image.

Exact Homepage visual composition details beyond this approved direction remain subject to faithful implementation of the mockup, responsive adaptation, and higher product/editorial authority where applicable.

## 5. MAIL ROOM BROWSE / ARCHIVE — APPROVED VISUAL DIRECTION

The approved Mail Room browse/archive visual direction includes:

- A **contemporary digital archive**, **not** a literal-room simulation.
- **THE MAIL ROOM** receives strong purple visual identity.
- Contemporary interface surrounds tactile correspondence artifacts.
- Historical artifacts may retain their actual age and material character.
- Contemporary correspondence must **not** be artificially aged.
- Browse/filter behavior is visually approved **in principle**.
- Exact filter taxonomy remains **unresolved**.
- Artifact-forward cards.
- Metadata is restrained and subordinate to the artifact.
- A visible **Write to Mail Room** affordance belongs in the experience.
- The archive should feel designed and alive without becoming a museum exhibit.

## 6. INDIVIDUAL CORRESPONDENCE DETAIL — APPROVED VISUAL DIRECTION

The approved correspondence-detail visual direction includes:

- The correspondence **artifact dominates**.
- Interface **recedes** around the artifact.
- Metadata is quiet and secondary.
- **Artifact-first, database-second.**
- A separate connection to Personal Seal, P.S. may appear when a real connection exists.
- **Zero connections is valid.**
- A **Write to Mail Room** affordance may remain available without becoming a conversion funnel.
- Historical source/archive/rights information required by the Product Model remains compatible with this visual direction.
