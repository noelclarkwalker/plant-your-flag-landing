# PORTAL STORYBOARD

## Purpose

This document defines the cinematic sequence of the Portal.

It does not define implementation.

It does not define engineering.

It defines only what the visitor experiences, moment by moment.

The Portal owns passage.

Emergence owns arrival.

The Portal ends before the Geography of Curiosity is revealed.

---

## Shot 001 — Stillness

The visitor reaches:

"I'm planting my flag."

Nothing moves.

Nothing changes.

Silence.

Duration: approximately three seconds.

**Purpose:** Conviction settles.

---

## Shot 002 — Recognition

The period remains ordinary punctuation.

The visitor's attention drifts.

When it returns, something feels wrong, although nothing has visibly changed.

**Purpose:** Doubt.

---

## Shot 003 — Impossible Depth

The period is not larger.

Not brighter.

Not glowing.

It simply possesses an inside.

The visitor cannot explain why.

**Purpose:** Curiosity.

---

## Shot 004 — Crossing

The visitor chooses to enter.

Nothing pulls them.

Nothing forces them.

Crossing is consent.

**Purpose:** Commitment.

---

## Shot 005 — Between

Darkness.

No horizon.

No orientation.

No destination.

No up.

No down.

The remembered world has ended.

The next world has not yet begun.

**Purpose:** Passage.

---

## Shot 006 — Threshold

The faintest suggestion that another world exists.

Nothing is yet identifiable.

No landscape is revealed.

No Geography is shown.

The visitor only senses that something lies beyond the darkness.

The Portal ends here.

**Purpose:** Anticipation.

---

## Permanent Boundary

The Portal never owns arrival.

Arrival belongs exclusively to Emergence.

The Geography of Curiosity is first revealed by Emergence, never by the Portal.
