# Prototype 001 — Scroll becomes Forward

**Status:** Strong candidate

### Question

Can scrolling stop feeling like webpage navigation and instead feel like physically moving toward the Portal?

### Conclusion

Yes.

The philosophy is strong.

The implementation still requires refinement, but the cinematic language appears promising.

### Keep

- Scroll naturally through the manifesto.
- Trigger only after the complete NC monogram is visible.
- Scroll becomes forward movement.
- Camera advances toward the monogram.
- The page becomes secondary.

### Improve

- Camera smoothing and inertia.
- Optical centering.
- Transition into Emergence.
- Final framing before Portal.

### User reaction

- Closest prototype to the intended vision so far.
- Camera movement feels promising.
- Movement should be smoother.
- Camera should advance farther before Emergence begins.
- Continue exploring this direction.

### Verdict

Continue exploring.
Do not commit to production yet.
