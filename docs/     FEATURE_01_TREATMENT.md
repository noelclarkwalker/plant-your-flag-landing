# FEATURE_01_TREATMENT

Black.

Nothing else.

Then, immediate white:

# PLANT YOUR FLAG

No logo.

No navigation.

No explanation.

Nothing moves.

The words simply exist against the black.

For a moment, there is only the statement.

**PLANT YOUR FLAG**

The visitor moves.

And the world begins.

---

The black gives way to something recognizable.

Not NoelClark.com yet.

Something familiar.

A feed.

A profile.

A post.

The ordinary language of the social internet.

Nothing announces the change.

The visitor simply finds themselves somewhere they already know how to navigate.

Borrowed land.

A social-media-inspired post sits before them.

Static.

Quiet.

Almost dry in its familiarity.

A real profile photograph.

**NOÈL CLARK**

A handle beneath it.

And writing:

**“There was a time when the internet felt like wandering into someone’s world...”**

The visitor reads.

Nothing competes with the words.

The familiarity does its work quietly.

Around the profile photograph, a soft glow remains.

No arrow points toward it.

No instruction explains it.

It simply draws the eye.

An invitation without language.

The visitor may follow it.

Or they may continue reading.

Either way, the invitation eventually finds them.

---

The glow surrounding the photograph begins to deepen.

Gently.

Almost imperceptibly at first.

Then it spreads.

Not like something overtaking a screen.

Like a flashback surfacing.

A memory coming back before the visitor quite knows what they are remembering.

The familiar social world begins to disappear beneath it.

Profile.

Post.

Feed.

The borrowed language recedes.

The glow carries the visitor with it.

And within it, another world begins to surface.

Color returns differently here.

The watercolor palette of the original NoelClark.com emerges from memory.

The visitor is not watching one place become another.

They are being carried backward into something remembered.

The social world is gone.

The writing remains.

And when the light settles, the visitor is somewhere else.

Somewhere older.

Somewhere personal.

Somewhere that feels remembered rather than newly revealed.

---

The visitor is inside a remembered NoelClark.com.

Not the website as it exists today.

The NoelClark.com that lives in memory.

The watercolor world of its earlier life surrounds the writing.

It feels inhabited.

Not reconstructed for the visitor.

Not waiting to introduce itself.

Already there.

The soft circular glow that first drew the visitor away from the feed remains quietly within the scene.

Still now.

No longer asking for attention.

The room holds the feeling of an internet before feeds decided what should come next.

Before everything needed an audience.

Before everything needed a metric.

A personal corner of the internet could simply be a personal corner of the internet.

The surroundings remember that.

But they do not speak for themselves.

The writing does.

---

The voice continues.

**“There was a time when the internet felt like wandering into someone’s world...”**

And deeper into the same thought:

**“NoelClark.com has been my home on the internet since 2010.”**

Then:

**“It was just me and my open journal...”**

The lines belong together.

No sentence asks to be admired.

The visitor simply reads.

The words were already here.

And gradually, what surrounds them begins to make sense.

This was not a profile.

Not a feed.

It was a home on the internet.

An open journal.

---

There is evidence of that life nearby.

An early NoelClark.com homepage.

A piece of 2012.

It does not demand attention.

It feels like an old photograph resting quietly in a room full of memory.

The visitor notices it.

An earlier version of this same home.

A homepage declaring a purpose to honor a late father’s life and help others.

The writing continues to lead.

The old homepage simply rests within the remembered world.

Proof without trying to prove anything.

This place has been lived in.

It has a past.

It remembers.

---

The visitor continues reading.

**“One day I’m writing about grief...”**

For a moment, the words carry the feeling of being written.

A typewriter.

Then the thought opens outward.

**“The next...”**

And more of the life behind the words becomes visible.

A deer.

Thelonious Monk.

Hip Hop.

Birds.

A farmers market.

Books.

Mathematics.

Nature.

Otis Redding.

Nipsey Hussle.

One memory catches the eye.

Then another.

Then another.

Never a gallery.

Never a parade.

Fragments of a life sharing the same room.

The writing has been telling the visitor who lives here.

Now the remembered world quietly answers.

---

The words arrive at:

**“Life doesn’t stay inside neat little categories.**

**Neither do I.”**

Something loosens.

There is more room.

More natural light.

More air.

The visitor feels the relief before thinking about why.

Grief can sit beside music.

A deer beside Hip Hop.

Books beside mathematics.

Nature beside memory.

An ordinary Tuesday afternoon beside the song that gave it a soundtrack.

Nothing needs to be sorted into a feed.

Nothing needs to become a category.

It all belongs here because it belongs to the same life.

The room can finally hold all of it.

---

The writing returns to the thing the visitor has already begun to remember.

There was a time when the internet felt like wandering into someone’s world.

Little corners that felt unmistakably their own.

Places you could visit.

Bookmark.

Return to.

Places that made the word **homepage** feel literal.

Then those homes became feeds.

Attention became something to monetize.

Expression became something to measure.

Lives and art moved onto borrowed land.

The visitor knows the difference now.

They entered through the feed.

They left it behind.

They have spent time inside what came before it.

Writing.

Memory.

Curiosity.

A person.

A home.

---

The words from the beginning return with their meaning intact.

**PLANT YOUR FLAG**

Now the writing answers them.

**“I don’t want to build on borrowed land anymore.”**

**“I’m planting my flag.”**

The sentence ends.

The period remains.

Small.

Certain.

For the briefest moment, it is simply the end of the sentence.

Then it begins to open.

Quietly.

The smallest possible mark becomes an opening.

Nothing announces what is happening.

Nothing needs to.

The declaration has made its own way forward.

---

The opening grows from that final period.

The familiar circle that first drew the visitor away from the feed is there again.

First an invitation.

Then stillness.

Now passage.

Light enters.

The remembered watercolor world remains behind it.

Ahead is something else.

The visitor moves through.

---

NoelClark.com.

Now.

The present-day home.

Journal.

Photography.

Art.

Music.

Curiosity.

Ideas.

Unexpected connections.

The visitor has arrived.

---

Navigation is here now.

**Journal**

**Art**

**About**

**John Clark**

**Contact**

No next step is chosen for them.

No path asks to be followed.

The website has finished leading.

Stay awhile.

**Wander.**
