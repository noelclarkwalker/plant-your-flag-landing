## Scene 02

The visitor enters familiar territory.

A social-media-inspired post.

Static.

Dry.

No tricks.

Real profile photograph.

**NOÈL CLARK**

Opening text:

**“There was a time when the internet felt like wandering into someone’s world...”**

## Scene 03

The glow surrounding the photograph deepens.

It gently overtakes the visitor’s view like a flashback surfacing in memory.

The social world recedes beneath it.

Profile.

Post.

Feed.

Borrowed language.

Within the glow, another world surfaces.

The watercolor palette of the original NoelClark.com returns.

The visitor is carried into a remembered NoelClark.com.

The social interface is gone.

The writing remains.

---

## Scene 04

The visitor is inside NoelClark.com as remembered.

Not the website as it exists today.

An earlier world.

Watercolor.

Personal.

Already inhabited.

The circular glow remains within the scene, completely still.

The writing continues:

**“NoelClark.com has been my home on the internet since 2010.”**

**“It was just me and my open journal...”**

The writing leads.

The visitor reads at their own pace.

The remembered world quietly holds the words.

---

## Scene 05

An original NoelClark.com homepage from 2012 is discovered within the remembered world.

It feels like an old photograph resting quietly nearby.

Not a gallery image.

Not decoration.

A memory object.

An earlier version of this same home.

Its presence establishes that NoelClark.com has a history.

The writing remains primary.

---

## Scene 06

The journal deepens.

**“One day I’m writing about grief...”**

Typewriter.

Only this passage carries that feeling of being written.

Then:

**“The next...”**

Memories begin to gather within the world.

A deer.

Thelonious Monk.

Hip Hop.

Birds.

Farmers markets.

Books.

Mathematics.

Nature.

Otis Redding.

Nipsey Hussle.

One memory is noticed.

Then another.

Then another.

They feel like memories rather than a slideshow.

Fragments of one life sharing the same space.

---

## Scene 07

The writing reaches:

**“Life doesn’t stay inside neat little categories.**

**Neither do I.”**

The remembered world slowly opens.

Natural light increases.

There is more room.

More air.

The visitor feels relief before consciously noticing why.

Grief can sit beside music.

A deer beside Hip Hop.

Books beside mathematics.

Nature beside memory.

An ordinary Tuesday afternoon beside the song that gave it a soundtrack.

Nothing needs to become a category.

It all belongs to the same life.

---

## Scene 08

The writing turns toward what changed.

There was a time when the internet felt like wandering into someone’s world.

Little corners that felt unmistakably their own.

Places people could visit.

Bookmark.

Return to.

Places that made the word **homepage** feel literal.

Those homes became feeds.

Attention became something to monetize.

Expression became something to measure.

Lives and art moved onto borrowed land.

The visitor has already experienced the difference.

They entered through the feed.

They left it behind.

They are standing inside the memory of what came before it.
