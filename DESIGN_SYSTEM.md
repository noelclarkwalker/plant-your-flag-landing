# NoelClark.com Design System

---

# Philosophy

This is not a website.

It is a home.

Visitors should feel like they are wandering through Noèl Clark's mind.

Story always comes first.

---

# Motion

The visitor always initiates movement.

Nothing animates simply because it can.

Motion reinforces meaning.

Subtle is stronger than flashy.

Wonder is discovered.

Never forced.

# Rules

No page builders.

No unnecessary animation.

No autoplay.

No decorative motion.

Photography tells the truth.

Writing leads.

Design supports.

Curiosity wins.

# Non-Negotiables

PLANT YOUR FLAG appears immediately.

No animation before user scrolls.

Navigation is globally available from initial arrival. Feature 01 unfolds beneath persistent navigation.

The user always initiates movement.

Every animation must have narrative purpose.

Nothing should resemble a template.

The writing is always the primary artwork.
