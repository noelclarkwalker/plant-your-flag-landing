# NoelClark.com

## Philosophy

This is not a website.

It is a home.

Visitors should never feel like they are navigating software.

They should feel like they are wandering through someone's mind.

Curiosity leads.

Every animation must support the writing.

Every photograph must tell the truth.

Restraint is part of the design.

Wonder is discovered, never forced.
If the browser proves us wrong...

we change the code.

Not the vision.

For production code:

Generate one complete file at a time.

Do not split production files across multiple responses.

Do not provide partial implementations.

If a file is too large, tell me before beginning so we can dedicate a new chat to that file.

## Feature 3 Design Notes (Draft)

The social post remains intentionally static.

The profile story ring gently pulses after the visitor has had time to read.

Visitors may click the story immediately.

If they do not, the story opens automatically after a short pause.

The story sequence gradually sheds the visual language of social media until nothing remains except the final flag.

The flag becomes the threshold into NoelClark.com.

The visitor does not arrive at another webpage.

The visitor crosses into a home.

The interface is gradually removed.

The home is gradually revealed.

Motion should feel architectural rather than cinematic.

Every transition should remove something rather than add something.

The escape from borrowed land is where NoelClark.com begins.

### Open Design Questions

• Exactly how long should the story sequence last?

• Should the stories auto-advance or respond to user taps after opening?

• How does the final flag physically become the threshold?

• What is the very first object revealed after crossing the threshold?

• At what exact moment does navigation first become available?
