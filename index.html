PLANT YOUR FLAG

There was a time when there were no algorithms monetizing your attention.

It was just people creating little corners of the internet that felt unmistakably their own, places you could visit, bookmark, and return to.

Somewhere along the way, we traded those homepages for feeds that fed us whatever highly processed content the machine needed us to consume. We built our lives on borrowed land and handed our art over to someone else's terms of service, hoping tomorrow's algorithm would remember we existed.

I don't want to build on borrowed land anymore.

I'm planting my flag.
