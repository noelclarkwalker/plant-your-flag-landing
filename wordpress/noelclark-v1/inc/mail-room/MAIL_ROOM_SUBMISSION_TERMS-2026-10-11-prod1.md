# THE MAIL ROOM SUBMISSION TERMS

These Submission Terms apply when you send a letter or other material to The Mail Room on NoelClark.com.

## 1. YOU MUST BE 18 OR OLDER

The Mail Room accepts submissions only from people who are at least 18 years old.

By submitting, you confirm that you are 18 or older.

## 2. YOUR LETTER IS YOURS

You retain copyright in the original material you submit.

Submitting something to The Mail Room does not transfer ownership of your copyright to Noèl Clark or NoelClark.com.

Your sharing selection determines what permission you give for your submission to be considered for public use.

## 3. CHOOSE HOW YOUR LETTER MAY BE SHARED

You will choose one of three permissions when submitting:

### NOT FOR PUBLIC SHARING

Your submission is not authorized for public sharing.

The Mail Room may receive and review it, but your submitted words will not be published as a Mail Room letter based on this submission.

### SHARE ANONYMOUSLY

Your submission may be considered for public sharing without publicly identifying you.

If selected, some or all of the authorized submission may appear publicly without your name or other identifying information.

### SHARE WITH MY NAME

Your submission may be considered for public sharing with the credit you provide.

Your public credit may be your real name, first name, initials, or a pseudonym.

Information you provide privately for administrative purposes, including your email address, is not automatically part of your public credit.

## 4. SHARING PERMISSION DOES NOT GUARANTEE PUBLICATION

Choosing SHARE ANONYMOUSLY or SHARE WITH MY NAME gives permission for your submission to be considered for public use. It does not require The Mail Room to publish it.

Submitting a letter does not guarantee:

* publication;
* selection;
* a public response;
* a private response;
* advice;
* ongoing correspondence;
* an explanation of why something was or was not selected; or
* notification that a submission was reviewed or selected.

No public outcome is a legitimate outcome.

## 5. EDITORIAL USE

If you authorize public sharing and your submission is selected, you give NoelClark.com permission to reproduce, display, publish, format, and make reasonable editorial changes to the authorized submission for presentation on NoelClark.com and its associated presentation of The Mail Room.

Reasonable editorial changes may include correcting obvious typographical errors, formatting paragraphs, adjusting capitalization or punctuation, shortening excerpts, and redacting private, identifying, sensitive, legally problematic, or irrelevant information.

Editorial changes will not intentionally alter the meaning of your submission or falsely attribute statements to you that you did not make.

This permission applies only to material you authorized for public sharing. It does not authorize publication of the words of a submission marked NOT FOR PUBLIC SHARING.

## 6. WORDS AND ARTIFACTS ARE DIFFERENT PERMISSIONS

Permission concerning the text of a submission does not automatically authorize public reproduction of every file or artifact submitted with it.

Photographs of letters, handwriting, scans, screenshots, artwork, photographs, recordings, attachments, and other submitted materials may involve separate rights and permissions.

Where NoelClark.com requests separate permission concerning an artifact or attachment, that choice controls whether that material may be publicly reproduced.

## 7. PRIVATE CONTACT INFORMATION

Your name and email are just for my private records. They won't be shown publicly unless you chose to be credited, and submitting a letter doesn't sign you up for emails or mean you'll receive a reply.

## 8. INSPIRATION AND INDEPENDENT CREATIVE WORK

Sending something to The Mail Room does not give the sender ownership over Noèl Clark's independent thoughts, ideas, research, observations, interests, questions, themes, concepts, discoveries, or creative work.

A submission may intersect with subjects Noèl was already thinking about, researching, writing about, or creating around.

The fact that a submission and later independent work share a subject, idea, experience, fact, question, theme, mood, or concept does not by itself mean that the later work reproduces or derives from the submission.

Permission to publish a submission and Noèl's ability to create independent original work are separate issues.

Nothing in these terms authorizes publication of the actual words of a submission marked NOT FOR PUBLIC SHARING.

## 9. OTHER PEOPLE'S RIGHTS

You should submit only material you have the right to submit.

Do not submit material in a way that violates another person's copyright, privacy, publicity rights, or other legal rights.

If your submission contains private or identifying information about another person, its inclusion does not automatically mean that information will be published.

NoelClark.com may withhold, redact, decline, or remove material where appropriate for privacy, safety, legal, or editorial reasons.

## 10. MODERATION

The Mail Room is curated.

NoelClark.com may decline, withhold, edit where authorized, restrict, or remove material for editorial, privacy, safety, legal, technical, or moderation reasons.

Submitting material does not create a right to have it hosted or kept on NoelClark.com.

## 11. WITHDRAWAL AND REMOVAL

You may request withdrawal of a submission before it has been published. If the request is received and can reasonably be matched to your submission before publication, NoelClark.com will not subsequently publish the submitted material under the permission granted through that submission.

If your authorized submission has already been published on NoelClark.com, you may request that it be removed from NoelClark.com. NoelClark.com will review and act on a valid removal request within a reasonable period of time.

Removal from NoelClark.com cannot guarantee removal of copies, screenshots, archives, search-engine records, quotations, or other reproductions that may have been created or distributed by third parties while the material was publicly available.

Withdrawal or removal of a submission does not give the sender ownership of, or require deletion or withdrawal of, Noèl Clark's independently created original work merely because that work concerns a similar subject, idea, experience, fact, question, theme, mood, or concept.

Nothing in this section converts a submission marked NOT FOR PUBLIC SHARING into material authorized for publication.

## 12. PRIVACY AND DATA

NoelClark.com may collect and store information you provide through The Mail Room, including your submission, sharing choice, name, email address, public credit if provided, records of your agreement to these Submission Terms, and technical or administrative information reasonably necessary to operate and protect the submission system.

This information may be used to receive and review submissions, administer The Mail Room, maintain records of permissions and sharing choices, communicate with you when reasonably necessary concerning your submission, prevent abuse, maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Submitting a letter does not subscribe you to marketing emails or a mailing list.

Private contact information is not made public merely because you submitted it. If you choose SHARE WITH MY NAME, only the public credit you provide for that purpose is authorized as your public credit.

Information may be processed or stored using service providers that support the operation, hosting, security, email delivery, or technical administration of NoelClark.com. Those services may process information as necessary to provide their services.

NoelClark.com will retain submission and permission records for as long as reasonably necessary to operate The Mail Room, document permissions and submission history, resolve disputes, protect the site and its users, and meet applicable legal obligations. Information that is no longer reasonably necessary should be deleted or de-identified where appropriate, subject to legitimate recordkeeping, backup, security, and legal requirements.

Reasonable measures will be used to protect information maintained through The Mail Room. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

By submitting a letter, you confirm that:
