# NERV_TREATMENT.md

# NoelClark.com Version 1.0
## Governing Creative Authority

---

# Purpose

This document defines the complete creative authority for the NERV Homepage room.

It governs the visitor's experience from the moment NERV becomes the active Homepage room until the visitor naturally progresses toward the following room.

It is not an engineering document.

It intentionally avoids prescribing implementation techniques, technologies, animation libraries, or engineering solutions.

The creative experience is authoritative.

The implementation is not.

---

# Investigation Begins With Choice

The visitor begins the act of investigation.

The room completes it.

NERV should reward curiosity.

It should never require persistence.

---

# Investigation Is Assisted

The visitor initiates investigation.

The room responds by supporting that curiosity.

NERV should never require visitors to search randomly or perform unnecessary effort.

Curiosity is rewarded through thoughtful guidance rather than challenged through difficulty.

---

# Engineering Independence

This document defines creative authority only.

It intentionally avoids prescribing implementation techniques, technologies, libraries, or engineering solutions.

Multiple technical implementations may satisfy this authority.

The creative experience is authoritative.

The implementation is not.

---

# Narrative Purpose

NERV teaches the visitor that understanding requires attention.

The room is not about hidden secrets.

It is about learning to look more carefully.

Knowledge already exists.

Understanding comes through observation.

---

# Emotional Objective

The room should communicate:

- curiosity
- investigation
- patience
- clarity
- discovery

The visitor should feel rewarded for slowing down.

The room should never feel like solving a puzzle.

Instead, it should feel like noticing something meaningful that had always been present.

---

# The Nature Of Discovery

Nothing within NERV is created by the visitor.

Nothing magically appears.

The visitor simply becomes capable of seeing what was already there.

The room teaches perception rather than revelation.

---

# Curiosity Is Rewarded

NERV does not ask visitors to prove their curiosity.

It simply asks them to express it.

The smallest act of investigation should be met with a meaningful response.

Visitors should never struggle to uncover the room's message.

Instead, the room should recognize their curiosity and reward it through thoughtful guidance.

Understanding should feel earned through attention rather than effort.

---

# Natural Affordance

The magnifying glass should naturally invite interaction through its physical presence.

Visitors should instinctively recognize that it can be picked up and used.

The room should communicate this through composition, lighting, shadow, proportion, and placement rather than explicit instruction.

Curiosity should begin naturally.

---

# Assisted Investigation

Once the visitor chooses to investigate, the room quietly supports that decision.

The visitor initiates the investigation.

The room completes it.

Investigation should never become work.

The visitor should feel guided without feeling directed.

---

# Lasting Impression

After leaving NERV, visitors should not remember operating a magnifying glass.

They should remember discovering that slowing down allowed them to see something more clearly.

NERV succeeds when visitors leave with a renewed appreciation that curiosity begins with paying closer attention.
---

# Opening Composition

NERV arrives as a complete cinematic room.

Its poster dominates the visitor's attention.

The room feels intelligent, quiet, and confident.

Nothing immediately asks for interaction.

The visitor should first observe.

---

# Hero Object

The magnifying glass is the hero object.

Everything else exists to support it.

The visitor should naturally understand that the magnifying glass is meant to be used.

The room should never require explicit instruction.

Curiosity should become the invitation.

---

# Investigation

Investigation is the room's language.

The visitor should actively participate in discovering meaning.

The room should never perform the discovery on the visitor's behalf.

Understanding belongs to the visitor.

---

# The Blurb

The blurb exists throughout the experience.

It is not introduced.

It is not animated into existence.

It becomes understandable through investigation.

The visitor should feel that the message was always waiting to be noticed.

---

# Trust

NERV trusts the visitor's intelligence.

The room should never overexplain itself.

It should quietly assume that visitors are capable of observation, curiosity, and thoughtful discovery.

The room respects their attention by rewarding it rather than demanding it.

---

# Reading Rhythm

The visitor experiences:

Observation

↓

Curiosity

↓

Investigation

↓

Discovery

↓

Understanding

↓

Invitation

Reading is not separate from investigation.

Reading is the investigation.

---

# Motion

Movement exists only to support investigation.

Nothing should move simply to attract attention.

The room should remain predominantly still.

Any movement should reinforce the act of careful observation.

---

# Environmental Presence

The surrounding environment should support concentration.

No supporting detail should distract from investigation.

Everything within the room should quietly reinforce thoughtful observation.

---

# Visual Hierarchy

The visitor's attention naturally progresses:

Poster

↓

Magnifying Glass

↓

Discovered Message

↓

Invitation

No competing focal points should interrupt this progression.

---

# The Visitor Is Never Rushed

The room rewards patience.

Visitors should never feel pressured to hurry toward discovery.

The experience should naturally encourage slowing down.

Understanding should arrive through attention rather than speed.

---

# Supporting Elements

Supporting objects exist only to reinforce investigation.

Nothing should compete with the magnifying glass for attention.

The hero object always remains visually dominant throughout the room.

---

# Invitation

Only after the visitor has understood the room's message should they be invited to enter NERV.

The invitation should feel like the natural conclusion of investigation.

It should never interrupt discovery.

---

# Transition

Leaving NERV should feel like carrying a new way of seeing into the following room.

The visitor progresses naturally.

Nothing should feel abrupt.

Nothing should feel mechanically segmented.

---

# Reduced Motion

Visitors who prefer reduced motion should experience the same emotional progression.

Movement may be simplified or removed.

The act of investigation must remain intact.

---

# Accessibility

Every visitor should experience investigation regardless of interaction method or assistive technology.

The room's emotional experience should remain consistent.

---

# Performance

Performance is part of the creative experience.

NERV should always feel immediate, responsive, and effortless.

Creative ambition should never compromise clarity or responsiveness.

---

# What Must Never Happen

NERV must never feel like:

- a puzzle game
- a hidden-object game
- an escape room
- a magic trick
- a microscope simulation
- a technology demonstration
- a gimmick

It is thoughtful observation.

Nothing more.

---

# Emotional Memory

After leaving NERV, visitors should not primarily remember moving a magnifying glass.

They should remember the feeling of discovering something by paying closer attention.

The mechanics should quietly disappear behind the experience.

---

# Success

NERV succeeds when visitors leave the room with a greater appreciation for careful observation.

The room should remind them that curiosity is not about finding hidden things.

It is about seeing familiar things more deeply.
