# EMERGENCE ASSET PLAN

## Status

Production authority for Emergence visual assets — NoelClark.com Version 1.0

This document defines **what must exist** before Emergence engineering resumes.

It does not define implementation, scenes, transitions, camera behavior, or Portal modifications.

It is the authoritative checklist for asset creation.

---

## Purpose

Emergence requires a complete visual inventory whose parts can coexist **without ever forcing the visitor to feel that a new scene loaded.**

Assets must support the governing discovery:

> *I was already moving into this world.*

The first recognizable element may be a **flower** — not because the Geography is botanical, but because a flower naturally bridges watercolor pigment and physical reality. The flower is the first object the brain successfully classifies. **The world must remain larger than the flower.**

Asset creation must therefore prioritize:

- **Continuity** over reveal
- **Relationship** over category
- **Local legibility** over establishing shots
- **Intentional curiosity objects** over decorative architecture

---

## Governing Constraints

### Must support

- Darkness inherited from Portal
- Progressive perception (night → dawn → daylight → spatial overexposure → white)
- Organic coexistence of unrelated subjects
- Bounded exploration of a world larger than what is shown
- Signal / Perception / Relationship interactive vocabulary (Radio, Camera, Record Player)
- Open Echoes that may remain unresolved

### Must avoid

Environmental shorthand that classifies the Geography too early:

- fantasy garden
- enchanted forest
- medieval village
- botanical garden
- fairy landscape
- pastoral establishing landscape
- nature-documentary scenery

Decorative or ornamental human-made **structures** unless later given explicit creative authority:

- gazebos
- cottages
- pavilions
- decorative bridges
- ornamental architecture

### Human-made rule

Human-made **objects** are welcome when they express curiosity — books, instruments, lenses, sound tools, art materials, scientific objects. Each must feel **inhabited and intentionally placed**, not styled as set dressing.

### Authenticity rule (locked)

No AI-generated depiction of Noèl or family members. Real photographs, video, artwork, and archival material whenever people appear.

---

## Asset Philosophy

### Continuity-first inventory

Every asset category below is organized to answer one question:

**Can this exist inside the same continuous forward movement begun at the Portal monogram — without requiring a cut, fade, dissolve, or new composition?**

Assets that imply a **genre**, **biome**, or **destination** are deferred or abstracted until relationship logic is established.

### Production tiers

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Hero** | Must exist for Emergence to be possible at all |
| **Supporting** | Required for environmental arc, exploration, or coherence |
| **Organic** | Living systems and natural material — present but never the subject |
| **Human-made curiosity** | Objects that express curiosity, not decoration |
| **Lighting** | Light behavior and illumination states — not scenery |
| **Depth** | Layered material that supports impossible depth without scene change |

### Production method

| Method | Use when |
|---|---|
| **Photograph (Noèl)** | Authentic objects, textures, memory, handwriting, real light on real matter |
| **Illustrate** | Continuity bridges, withheld mass, impossible coexistence, non-photographic pigment logic |
| **Abstract until later** | Anything that would prematurely classify the world or require unresolved creative authority |

# 6. Day / Night Asset Strategy

## 6.1 One Geography, Two Legibility States

Night and day are **not separate asset worlds.** They are different perceptual states of the same inventory.

| Strategy | Application |
|---|---|
| **Dual-pass photography** | Hero objects photographed in controlled low light + daylight |
| **Derived legibility passes** | Same object, increased information (titles, handwriting, blossom detail) |
| **Silhouette → recognizable pairs** | Hawk, deer, instruments — night partial, day specific |
| **Withheld → revealed pairs** | Water ripple behavior, magnolia blossom, journal ink |
| **Independent time** | Dawn/shower/overexposure assets exist regardless of visitor path |

## 6.2 Minimum Dual-State Coverage

Every hero and supporting object in sections 1–2 needs at minimum:

1. **Night-partial** — readable only locally or as silhouette  
2. **Day-legible** — material truth available in daylight arc  

Exceptions: objects intentionally never fully understood during Emergence remain **abstract until later**.

## 6.3 Environmental Arc Asset Checklist

| Arc stage | Asset state |
|---|---|
| Darkness inherited | Void + occupied mass + continuity surface |
| Fragmentary light | Firefly-lit fragments of existing objects |
| Moonlight | Selective passes on existing objects + vertical mass |
| Local illumination | Chandelier pools on existing objects |
| Dawn | Wet variants + ambient shift on **same** objects |
| Daylight | Legibility passes — text, blossom, ripple, wildlife detail |
| Broadened awareness | More of same inventory perceivable — not new inventory drop |
| Full illumination | Overexposure consumption of same inventory |
| White | Typography only — Geography visually indistinguishable |

# 7. Depth Layers Needed

Depth supports **impossible depth without scene change** — object class remains; one property breaks.

Layers are **compositional material**, not separate scenes.

| Layer | Role | Contents | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L0 — Inherited pigment** | Portal continuity | Watercolor wash, bleed, paper tooth matching monogram | Illustrate |
| **L1 — Near field** | First feet of perception | Ground/mineral surface, flower base, water edge, object resting planes | Photograph (Noèl) |
| **L2 — Mid field** | Curiosity objects | Books, instruments, bowl, journal, record player, camera, radio | Photograph (Noèl) |
| **L3 — Local light origin** | Off-frame or partial | Chandelier fragment, firefly positions, candle reference if approved | Photograph / illustrate |
| **L4 — Vertical withheld mass** | Height without breadth | Trunk edges, hawk perch support, chain into void | Photograph fragments + illustrate |
| **L5 — Occupied darkness** | Infinity without establishing | Unclassifiable void — outdoor scale implied, never shown | Illustrate |
| **L6 — Sky / ambient** | Arc progression only | Night absent → dawn partial → day bright → overexposure | Abstract until needed per shot authority |
| **L7 — Memory plane** | Wayback / photography as object | Partial memory plates within coexistence | Photograph (Noèl) archival |

### Parallax / stratigraphy reference material

For illusion experiments (Prototype 009 direction), produce **photographed or illustrated layer separations** of:

- Monogram pigment strata (reference only — Portal asset)
- Flower + ground contact plane
- Open book pages vs cover
- Water surface vs stone beneath

These are **reference plates for depth research**, not Emergence scene builds.

# 11. Definition of Done

Asset production for Emergence threshold experience is complete enough for engineering when:

1. **Every hero asset** in Section 1 exists in approved form  
2. **Every supporting category** in Section 2 has minimum viable inventory  
3. **Organic inventory** is fragment-based — no biome establishing sets exist  
4. **Day/night dual passes** exist for all hero and supporting objects  
5. **Depth layer reference material** exists for continuity research  
6. **Deferred list** has not been prematurely produced  
7. No asset in inventory requires the visitor to feel **Scene B loaded**

Engineering may resume only when this checklist is satisfied — not when a prototype scene compiles.
