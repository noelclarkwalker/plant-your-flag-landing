# MANIFESTO_TREATMENT.md

# Purpose

The Manifesto defines the emotional heart of NoelClark.com.

It begins immediately after the remembered social world begins dissolving away and concludes with the author's signature.

It is neither a journal entry nor a landing page.

It is a declaration.

This document defines only the visitor's emotional and visual experience.

It does not define engineering, implementation, animation libraries, or Portal mechanics.

---

# Emotional Goal

The Manifesto should feel inevitable rather than animated.

Every paragraph carries slightly more emotional weight than the one before it until the visitor arrives naturally at:

"I don't want to build on borrowed land anymore."

followed by:

"I'm planting my flag."

The visitor should never feel persuaded.

They should feel that every paragraph removes another layer of distraction until only conviction remains.

---

# What the Visitor Experiences

The visitor continues scrolling naturally out of the remembered social world.

There is no page transition.

There is no interruption.

The social interface quietly dissolves away until only the words remain.

The Manifesto feels as though it had always existed beneath the temporary noise of the platform.

The visitor reads at their own pace.

Nothing controls their timing.

Nothing rushes them.

Nothing performs for them.

The experience belongs entirely to the visitor.

---

# Visual Language

The Manifesto exists against a deep black background.

The black should never feel digitally flat.

A nearly imperceptible vignette gives the page quiet depth without creating a visible spotlight.

An extremely subtle organic grain gives the surface the feeling of photographed paper rather than pure digital black.

No decorative elements exist.

No dividers.

No ornaments.

No unnecessary graphics.

The words alone create the composition.

---

# Typography

The approved production typography is permanently preserved.

Do not redesign:

- font family
- hierarchy
- spacing
- composition
- layout

Only the atmosphere surrounding the typography may evolve.

The closing declarations carry slightly greater visual authority than the preceding paragraphs.

This increase should feel natural rather than dramatic.

---

# Motion Philosophy

Motion exists only to deepen attention.

Never to entertain.

Never to impress.

Never to compete with the words.

The visitor remains in complete control through scrolling.

As each paragraph reaches its natural reading position, it quietly resolves into complete clarity.

The effect should feel like ink settling into paper rather than a digital animation.

Any movement should remain almost imperceptible.

If parallax exists at all, it should be subtle enough to be felt rather than consciously noticed.

No autoplay is ever permitted.

---

# Relationship to the Social World

The Manifesto grows out of the remembered social world.

The social platform is temporary.

The words are permanent.

As the visitor continues scrolling, only social-media artifacts dissolve:

- profile cards
- avatars
- reactions
- comments
- interface chrome
- platform framing

The writing remains.

Nothing slides.

Nothing explodes.

Nothing flies away.

The interface quietly disappears until only the declaration remains.

---

# Relationship to the Portal

The Manifesto concludes before the Portal begins.

Nothing within the Manifesto hints at, reveals, or begins the Portal.

Only after the declaration has fully concluded does the visitor encounter Noel Clark's watercolor maker's mark.

The approved asset:

`assets/images/nc-monogram-watercolor.png`

serves as Noel's signature.

The signature marks the completion of the declaration.

The Portal begins only after this point, under the authority of `PORTAL_TREATMENT.md`.
